**Status:** DRAFT. Hackathon Innovate4Cities deadline **31.8.2026 PASSED** (as of 2026-09-06). This doc is for portfolio / future municipal outreach only.

## Problem
Drivers waste time circling for curb parking; cities lack a cheap, privacy-first way to measure occupancy agreement between a local heuristic and a human inspector.

## City framing (current demo)
- **Named in kit:** Netanya (הרצל and sample lots) — illustrative, not a signed pilot.
- **Claim boundary:** browser-local demo + synthetic patches. No live municipal feed.

## What is proven in-repo
- Synthetic labeled scenes + v1/v2 heuristic comparison.
- CSV export with stable SHA-256 golden checksum for occupancy rows.
- Paired inspector workflow pages under `pilot-*.html`.

## What is NOT proven
- Field accuracy vs Netanya inspectors.
- Operational SLAs, procurement, or funding.
- Camera / ANPR integration.
